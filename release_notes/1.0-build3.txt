Highway signs have a new look. When it is time to get on or off a highway, the top banner now shows a small drawing of the ramp leaving or joining the road, next to the sign on green Liquid Glass with the same wording as the real sign.

The glass panels across the app are more see-through again, so the map shows through the banner and the trip card while the text stays easy to read.

To try the highway signs, swipe the bottom card up and switch Demo ride to Drive.
