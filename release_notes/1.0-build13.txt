Turn directions now come up based on how fast you are going: about a mile ahead at 45 mph and two miles ahead at 65 mph and above, with city timing at lower speeds. When a direction comes up early on the highway, the voice reminds you again just before the turn.

The posted speed limit now shows at the top left, under the direction banner, using the limits from OpenStreetMap. Where a road has no posted limit on record, the sign stays hidden.

The highway entrance drawing is fixed: it now shows the ramp leaving your road on the side you turn. Searching for a place also prefers the closest match to you.
