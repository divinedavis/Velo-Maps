Voice guidance now uses a natural ElevenLabs voice instead of the built-in iPhone voice. It speaks each turn, for example "In 300 feet, turn left onto West 96th Street", reads street names in full, and says "Pothole ahead" and your stop alert.

The voice is built into the app, so it works without a data connection. It still lowers your music while it speaks, and you can turn it off in the settings card.
