The Home screen now works like Apple Maps. Search sits at the bottom of the map in a sheet you can drag up to half or full height.

Tap the search field to see category shortcuts, your recent places, suggested places and restaurants nearby with Look Around photos.

You can save Home, Work and pinned places, and they stay on your phone. Recents can be opened as a full list and cleared.

A new map button switches between Explore, Driving and Satellite, with a traffic toggle. There are also buttons for 2D or 3D and for jumping back to where you are.

The route preview now has Look Around and Add to Places.
